SIMCO PMS — Privacy

SIMCO (Shoukat Iqbal Mir & Co.) Pvt Ltd · Last updated 21 October 2026

SIMCO PMS is the company's internal project management system. It is used only by SIMCO staff, each with an account issued by the company. It is not offered to the public.

Google Drive

The system is connected to the company's own Google Drive account, where project documents have always been kept. It uses that access for one purpose: to store the documents and vendor quotations that staff upload against a project, in that project's folder, and to move a document to the Drive bin when staff remove it. It does not read, change or share anything else in the Drive, and it does not access any other Google account.

What is kept, and where

The system keeps the company's own business records — projects, purchasing, stock, billing and the people who work on them — in a database run for the company. Files stay in the company's Google Drive. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
